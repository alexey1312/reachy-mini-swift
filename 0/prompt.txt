**Can it coexist with the 3D scene? No — by construction.** `ViewportModel` (`ViewportModel.swift:13-237`) owns *both* and guarantees exactly one runs: `activate()` (L152-180) starts one engine and stops/pauses the other; `content: Content` is `.scene | .camera` (L16-37). `ViewportOptions.offered(by:offersCamera:)` (`ViewportContent.swift:58-63`) is what both hosts' switchers render. So "camera *and* scene at once" would need a second `ViewportModel` (and for the scene, a second `RobotSceneModel` — `Sources/ReachyScene/AGENTS.md:6-7`: one `Entity` has one parent, **exactly one live `RealityView` per `RobotSceneModel`**).
