ABOUT THE APP

Hey Reachy is an unofficial network client for the Reachy Mini desktop robots from Pollen Robotics. It is not affiliated with Pollen Robotics. The app speaks to the robot's own HTTP and WebSocket daemon. This is the first native Apple Vision Pro version. Before it, Apple Vision ran the iPad app.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT A ROBOT

The app carries a simulator of the control path. It needs no robot, no account, no network and no permission.

1. On the Connect screen, open "Developer" below the list.
2. Tap "Start the simulator".
3. The app builds a robot from its own description and opens the main tabs. The tab bar is at the leading edge of the window.

HEAD TRACKING (NEW ON APPLE VISION PRO)

The headset's own pose steers the robot's head. To see it with the simulator:

1. Start the simulator as above.
2. Open the Live tab (the cube icon, second in the tab bar). The simulated robot is asleep. Tap "Wake up". The 3D robot appears.
3. Tap the game controller button in the top trailing corner of the window. The Controller screen opens.
4. Under "Head tracking", switch on "Follow my head". Look straight ahead when you do: that direction becomes straight ahead for the robot.
5. visionOS gives the headset's position only in an immersive space, so the app opens an empty one. The room and the app's window stay in view; other apps' windows hide.
6. Go back to the Live tab and turn, nod or tilt your head. The 3D robot's head follows, inside the reach of the robot's motors: 40 degrees to each side, 35 degrees down, 30 degrees up and 30 degrees of tilt. Its body does not turn.
7. Switch "Follow my head" off on the Controller screen, or press the Digital Crown to close the space. Either one puts the robot's head back to straight ahead.

The same switch is also a button beside the joystick on the Live tab.

Head tracking asks for no permission: the app reads only the device pose, and no camera, hand or surroundings data.

FIRST LAUNCH

A Bluetooth sheet opens over the Connect screen. Dismiss it with Cancel. The visionOS Local Network alert waits below it. Allow it, or use the simulator, which needs no permission.

WHAT NEEDS A REAL ROBOT

The camera, the two-way audio, the app catalogue, the moves, the guided first wake-up and the Wi-Fi setup over Bluetooth. The simulator declines them, because each one belongs to a physical machine.

Community apps (guideline 1.2): the Apps tab lists apps that authors publish on Hugging Face Spaces. Before Discover first shows them, a notice asks the user to agree, with a link to the Hugging Face content policy. Each app page and row menu has "Report this app" (the Hugging Face report form) and "Hide this author's apps"; "Hidden authors" undoes it.

DEMO ACCOUNT

The Hugging Face sign-in gives one function: remote access to a robot linked to that account. It is optional. All other functions work without an account.

The demo account has no robot linked to it, so after you sign in "Your Reachies" shows "No Reachy online". This is the correct result. No other content is behind the sign-in.

To sign in: select "Remote" on the Connect screen. Tap "Your Reachies", then "Sign in". Use the account in this section.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))

The app creates no account and has no server. The only sign-in is OAuth into an existing Hugging Face account. The app holds one access token in the Keychain, and "Sign out" deletes it.

Settings tab > "Hugging Face" section > "Delete your Hugging Face account" opens https://huggingface.co/settings/account, which carries the "Delete my account" control. The row appears as soon as the user signs in.

TWO-WAY AUDIO

On Apple Vision Pro the voice with the robot is not a system call: no LiveCommunicationKit and no "voip" background mode. The microphone button on the camera turns the microphone on and off.
